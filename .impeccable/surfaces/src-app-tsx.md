# Landing + studio (/)

Mode: Persuade (landing sections). The embedded studio follows Operate rules inside the same world.
Audience: mixed (see PRODUCT.md). Job: make a QR code that scans once printed, and leave with the file.
Primary action: edit the link in the hero job ticket and download. The full studio is one scroll below.
Proof: the live control strip (real contrast at the weakest point), a before/after correction with real renders, a distance ladder decoded live by jsQR, and a live count of cross-origin requests.
Constraints: no third-party requests, works offline, 320px, reduced motion, no invented claims. The scan model is labelled simulated.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Tessera is the press check a QR code never got: the code is proofed like a sheet before the run. It refuses the SaaS split hero with a glow, the badge row and the equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: One tall proof sheet of bright coated stock (#fbfbf9) lying on light-booth grey (#d6d6d1), in black ink (#111). Crop and registration marks mark the corners and section trims. A slug line across the top carries the job and the navigation. Process inks do jobs: magenta (#e4007f) for stamps, proofreader marks and the primary action; cyan (#00a0e9) for registration and info; yellow (#ffe600) only as a highlighter on warnings. Everything is set in Archivo, wide and heavy for display and tabular for readings. Ruled job-ticket tables replace cards. No shadows, glass or gradients outside the codes themselves.

STORY: The visitor sees their own link proofed live, understands that screen colours can fail on paper, watches Tessera catch it with real readings, then takes a file stamped for press.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The sheet fills the frame with crop marks at its corners. Slug line on top: Tessera, proof number and navigation. The left ~45% holds a giant three-line headline, "Proof the code before the run.", a one-line sub, a two-row job ticket (an editable job link and a print size) and a magenta "Pass for press" stamp that downloads. The right ~40% holds the live code at about 520px inside a dashed trim with registration targets. A full-width colour control strip runs along the bottom edge: first the code's real inks (ink, gradient end when there is one, paper), each with its reading, the weakest marked; then the symbol's measured readings (capacity, recovery, quiet zone, modules) in tint cells. A ticket row gives the print size (2 cm or larger). The stamp is magenta even on the sample, and before a link exists it focuses the job link.

FORM: Press Check, the prepress proof sheet. Candidate 1 of 7 on the ordered list (the IMPECCABLE'S PICK card). Seed key 202d2dcf. Signature interaction: the control strip re-reads on every edit, and the stamp presses with ink spread on download. Motion grammar: ink lands (scale 1.06 to 1 with an opacity settle) and the registration aligns; readings tick in fixed cells.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
